Not a weekend project

A year in the making.

Google reshuffles its results markup constantly, and a scrubber that guesses wrong hides the wrong thing. So this one is built carefully, in the open, and tested against the layouts Google actually ships. It's a real product, not a hobby.

It also stays out of your way. SearchScrub only wakes up on Google, does its work before the page paints, and keeps a running count of what it pulled so you can see it earning its place.
