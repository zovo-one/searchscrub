SearchScrub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-03

SearchScrub does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal or browsing data.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings, the curated + user-added blocklist, the lifetime scrubbed count, and the install date are stored locally in your browser's extension storage.
  • This data never leaves your device and is never synced to any server.

What SearchScrub accesses

  • The extension runs only on Google Search domains (google.com and five country TLDs: co.uk, ca, com.au, de, fr).
  • On those pages it reads the page DOM solely to hide the elements you asked it to hide (AI Overviews, AI Mode, People also ask, ads, and blocked domains).
  • It does not read, log, or transmit your search queries or results.

Network activity

  • SearchScrub makes no analytics, telemetry, or tracking requests of any kind.
  • The only outbound request is optional: if you activate a Pro license, your license key alone is sent once to the Zovo licensing endpoint to verify your subscription. No browsing data accompanies that request.

Your control

  • Every hiding feature can be toggled off from the popup.
  • Every blocked domain can be removed, and the whole blocklist reset or cleared, from the options page.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
